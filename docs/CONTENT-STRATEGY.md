### Content acceptance

- Original work and a concrete reader benefit; no filler or vendor-copied product marketing.
- Evidence-led statements linked to official specifications where needed.
- Byline, research type and update date; no fabricated first-hand tests.
- Fair pros, cons, suitability criteria and alternatives.
- Affiliate disclosure near actual paid links when introduced.
- No stale current prices or stock indicators.
