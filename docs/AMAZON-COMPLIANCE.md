# Amazon Associates launch checklist

Required declaration **only after confirmed active participation**: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.”

Use `PUBLIC_AMAZON_ASSOCIATE_ACTIVE=true` only after confirming enrollment; it defaults to false.

Phase 1 uses **no referral URLs, tracking ID, Amazon images or current Amazon prices**.

Before adding affiliate links:

1. Obtain and verify approval/eligibility for the site and account and register the exact live site in Associates Central.
2. Confirm current programme terms in India: https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/agreement
3. Verify linking rules: https://affiliate-program.amazon.in/help/operating/linking
4. Place clear disclosure close to each affiliate referral such as `(paid link)`.
5. Use approved product URLs and avoid cloaking or manipulating affiliate destinations.
6. Do not scrape Amazon reviews/images, show fabricated live prices or claim endorsement by Amazon.
7. Re-check programme rules before the first live affiliate placement and when they change.

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. Owner must review final language and applicable privacy obligations.
